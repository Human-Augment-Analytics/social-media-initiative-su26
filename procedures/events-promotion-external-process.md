# Procedure: External Event Promotion Process

## Purpose
This procedure is the workflow for promoting HAAG researchers, faculty, and computational advisors who are presenting research papers, speaking on panels, or hosting workshops at external conferences and academic venues. The  objective is to bring awareness within HAAG and outside of HAAG by posting on LinkedIn.

## Scope and Use
*   **When to Use:** This procedure must be initiated as the social media coordinator becomes aware that a HAAG member has an upcoming external presentation.
*   **What It Covers:** The slack tracker, communicating with the presenter, making a LinkedIn Post and recap LinkedIn post.


## Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Social Media Coordinator:** Takes the event details from the tracking list, requests necessary additional information from the presenters, drafts the LinkedIn posts using standard templates, goes through peer-review process, publishes the content, and drives internal engagement.
*   **Presenter (HAAG Researcher/Advisor):** Responsible for logging event data into the Slack tracker, providing information on request, and verifying accuracy of post drafts


## Tools or Prerequisites
1.  **Slack External Events Tracker:** An interactive Slack List hosted in `#haag-admin` used to log and filter ongoing campaigns. Link to list: https://humanaugmente-e7j6563.slack.com/lists/T071VTSFLCV

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Identify and Track the External Event
When any HAAG member becomes aware of an upcoming external event featuring themselves or other HAAG members, log the details into the Slack External Events Tracker <https://humanaugmente-e7j6563.slack.com/lists/T071VTSFLCV/F0BK3ET8KNH>.

Ensure the following information is captured so it is ready for the LinkedIn post:
*   Topic Title
*   Event Date
*   Time of Event
*   Location
*   Presenters
*   Summary of what will be discussed (why its important)
*   Link (if virtual)

### 2. Obtain extra details and images
Reach out to the presenter(s) via Slack or email to request a visual to accompany the post.

**Sample Message:** 
> *Hi [Name], I see you are presenting at [Event/Location] on [Date]! I am putting together a LinkedIn post to announce your upcoming presentation and invite people to attend. Could you please share a photo (a headshot, a figure/diagram from your project, or the official event flyer) that we can feature?*

*If Necessary*
> *Some information in the Slack Tracker was omitted. Could you please go to* <https://humanaugmente-e7j6563.slack.com/lists/T071VTSFLCV/F0BK3ET8KNH> *to help me in writing this post as soon as possible?*

### 3. LinkedIn Post Draft

**[Hook: 1-2 lines to grab attention regarding the topic or the presenter using the [Topic] part of the Slack Tracker]**
*Example:* How do you solve [Problem [Topic in Slack Tracker]]? HAAG researcher [Presenter Name] is taking our latest research on the road to [Location/Conference [Location in Slack Tracker]] to answer exactly that.

**[Visual]**
[Attach the photo of the presenter, project figure, or event flyer]

**[Body: The Basics & The Why]**
Join us on [Event Date] at [Time of Event] for an exciting presentation on "[Topic]." [Presenter Name] will be discussing [1-2 sentences on what the project does and why it matters using the [Summary of Slack Tracker]].

**[Credit Line/Tags]**

**[Close / Call to Action]**
If you are attending the event in [Location], be sure to check it out! You can also tune in virtually here: [Link]. Let us know in the comments if you plan on attending or want to learn more about this research!

#HAAG #GeorgiaTech [#Conference/Event Name]

### 4. Review Process and Official Posting
Before participating in the #Post-Feedback process, check-in with the presenters to verify the information in the post is all factually correct.

*   Share the draft in the `#post-feedback` Slack channel for team and stakeholder review. There is a separate process for how this review occurs.
*   Move the ‘LinkedIn Post Progress’ on Slack tracker to ‘HAAG Review’.
*   Once approved, the post can be officially published.
*   After publishing, the ‘LinkedIn Post Progress’ on Slack tracker should be changed to ‘Posted’.

### 5. Promote the Post Internally
:loudspeaker: Our latest event promotion post is now live!

Check out our newest post highlighting [Presenter Name] and their upcoming presentation at [Event/Conference Name]!

If you have a few minutes, please support the post with a :+1:, a comment, or a share—it really helps us increase the visibility of HAAG and our amazing researchers!

[Link to LinkedIn Post]

*(If applicable for virtual event)* You can also register to attend the event virtually here: [Link to register]

### 7. Slack Tracker Updates
Once the event finishes and the post event summary is posted (if applicable) the ‘Completed’ column may be checkmarked for tracking purposes.

## Outputs or Success Criteria
*   **Post inside HAAG Slack:** A call for HAAG to attend before the event date.
*   **LinkedIn Post before event:** Spreading awareness of event before event occurs. 
*   **LinkedIn Post after event:** Spreading summary of event after the event happens.
