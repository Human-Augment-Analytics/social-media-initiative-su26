# Procedure: Blog Post External Organization Email

## Purpose
This document provides the standardized email pitch template used to contact external organizations (such as partner universities, external departments, and PR writers) to pitch story collaborations. The goal is to highlight the interdisciplinary research that their affiliated researchers are conducting in partnership with the Human-Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG) at Georgia Tech.

## Scope and Use
Used for sending email out to an external organization to get them to create an article about HAAG. Improved email contains more information about HAAG and get the external organization to do the work.

## Roles and Responsibilities
Used by whomever is taking on the blog post work within HAAG.

## Tools or Prerequisites
Requires following the blog post template created by Neelima (not made by summer 2026 mgt semester). Requires information about the subject the blog post will be written about.

## Step-by-Step Process: Pitch Evolution

The following section outlines the structural shift from our legacy, passive outreach style to our highly active, persuasive "mutually beneficial" pitch format.

###  Outreach Pitch (Old Version)
Our old email format was overly brief, did not explain HAAG, and did not entice the reader on why they should take further action.

```text
Subject: Story Idea for Your Newsletter

Good morning [Name],

I recently enjoyed reading your article about [topic].

I thought you might be interested in a story about [research project, collaboration, student achievement, etc.].

Our team has been working on [brief description], and we believe the experience could be valuable and interesting to your audience because [reason].

Would you be interested in learning more?

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Organization]
[Email]

```

### Outreach Pitch (New Version)
Our updated, highly persuasive template immediately establishes HAAG's credentials, highlights the external institution's direct connection to the research, and presents a clear understanding of what they have to gain.

```text
Subject: Collaboration Story Pitch: [HAAG Member Name] & Georgia Tech

Good morning [Their Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am an admin for the Human-Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG) at Georgia Institute of Technology. We are an interdisciplinary research program that partners computational researchers with academic mentors and domain experts to drive applied research in areas like Generative AI, natural language processing, and data analytics.

I am a regular reader of [Newsletter/Blog Name] and really enjoyed your recent piece on [recent topic they covered].

I am reaching out because one of your own [HAAG Member Name], a [Member's Title/Role] at [External Organization] is currently driving incredible transdisciplinary research with our team at the Human-Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG) at Georgia Tech.

They are doing incredible work on [1-2 sentence brief description of the project]. This collaboration is unique because [explain the project's uniqueness or societal impact], and we believe your readers would love to see how a member of the [External Organization] community is leading the charge in this space.

We view this as a mutually beneficial partnership and want to help promote your organization's success. If you are interested in covering this, we already have a project summary and quotes from [HAAG Member Name] prepared for you. Furthermore, once you publish the piece, our team will amplify it by re-sharing your post and linking back to your site across our HAAG LinkedIn network and internal channels. This way, we can drive our audience directly to your platform so you benefit from the increased visibility.

Would you be interested in seeing if this story fits an upcoming feature?

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

[Your Name]  
[Your Role, HAAG]  
[Link to HAAG Website / Project Page]
```

## Success Criteria
The goal is to have the recipient agree to make an article.
