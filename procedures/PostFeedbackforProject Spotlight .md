# **Project Spotlight: Post Feedback and Approval Process**

## I. Title and Purpose

This is the Post-Feedback and Approval process document for the Project Spotlight. The purpose of this document is to provide a guide for reviewing and seeking approval for all LinkedIn draft posts in relation to the Project Spotlight. This series of steps, found within this document, helps make sure every draft gets feedback and reviews before being posted on LinkedIn.

## II. Scope and Use

The document covers any draft that is related to the Project Spotlight. This is from the primary draft to the final draft. The document only goes over getting feedback and approval in Slack. The document will not go over the other stages of the procedure pipeline.

## III. Roles and Responsibilities

- Draft Writer - Writes, edits, and uploads the draft into the Slack channel. Makes the announcement about the draft within the #post-feedback channel. Gathers all feedback received about the draft.
- Research group members- provides feedback and comments about the draft
- Team Led, Faculty, or Bree - approves the draft before it gets posted onto LinkedIn.

## IV. Tools or Prerequisites

- Slack
- Word Document
- Draft writer received access to the slack channel of the research group
- #post-feedback channel along with the specific folder for uploading the drafts.

## V. Step-by-Step Process

Please use this post-feedback and approval process for every single draft before anything gets posted on LinkedIn.

**Step 0:** The author of the draft will be invited into that specific research group’s Slack channel to start the edits of the post draft.

**Stage 1:** Upload the draft inside the #post-feedback channel in Slack. Please use the dedicated folder for the uploads of the drafts.

**Stage 2:** Then make an announcement in the #post-feedback channel when the draft is ready, along with providing instructions for how to make comments within the draft.

**Stage 3:** Make sure to allow a 48-hour period for annotations, revisions, and feedback about the draft.

**Stage 4:** Review and gather all the feedback by having a thread attached to the draft. This is for keeping the feedback organized and to reduce the number of DMs.

**Stage 5:** Finally, the writer of the draft will review and analyze all the feedback received by editing and updating the draft. Once the 48-hour period is complete, the draft must be approved by Bree, faculty, or a team leader before the draft is posted on LinkedIn.

## VI. Outputs/Success Criteria

The draft is prepared to be published on LinkedIn after the post has received feedback from the research members of the project spotlight and there is approval by a team leader, faculty, or Bree. This also must be captured in the feedback thread.

## VII. Lessons and Future Improvements

- Receiving feedback for the project spotlight was not a success.
- When posting a project spotlight draft into the slack it was very difficult to get even a response or feedback about the draft.
- Need to get make better procedures or protocols about this issue
- Need to set a proper deadline of when the feedback needs to be received by a certain time. Maybe 48 hours is not enough.
