# Procedure: External Event LinkedIn Post Review and Approval Process

## Purpose
This procedure defines the  review, verification, and approval workflow that each LinkedIn post  must undergo before it is published. Applying this process  prevents errors and maintains a professional presence.

## Scope and Use
*   **When to Use:** This process must be followed for every draft of the two external event LinkedIn posts (Before and After) before any content is uploaded to the official HAAG LinkedIn page.
*   **What It Covers:** Standardizes draft uploads, team feedback windows, feedback process, and final leadership sign-off.

## Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Writer of the Draft (Social Media Coordinator):** Responsible for obtaining more information from presenters, uploading the draft, announcing its availability, managing feedback within the thread, editing the draft, and securing final approval.
*   **Reviewers (HAAG Admins and Presenters):** Responsible for reviewing the draft and providing annotations, edits, and comments within the designated feedback window.
*   **Approvers (Bree or approved faculty):** Responsible for conducting the final review and giving the official green light to publish.

## Tools or Prerequisites
1.  **Slack Channel:** Access to the private `#post-feedback` channel in Slack.
2.  **Draft Content:** The information contained within the External Event Tracker and pictures from the presenters for the LinkedIn Post. External Event Tracker list found at: https://humanaugmente-e7j6563.slack.com/lists/T071VTSFLCV/F0BK3ET8KNH

## Step-by-Step Process

This five-stage pipeline must be executed sequentially for both the **Before** and **After** LinkedIn drafts:

### Stage 1: Presenter Verification
Verify with the presenters that the drafted text, technical details, and accompanying visual assets are completely accurate and acceptable.

### Stage 2: Presenter Onboarding
Bring the presenters into the private `#post-feedback` Slack channel so they are directly accessible to answer any additional feedback questions, comments, or concerns raised by the review team.

### Stage 3: Alert Admins
Post the draft in the `#post-feedback` channel to alert admins that the post is ready for review. Add the draft document to the message and instruct reviewers to ask their questions and leave feedback exclusively within the Slack thread of that post.

### Stage 4: Revisions and Feedback Window
Allow a strict 48-hour period for annotations, revisions, and feedback. The writer of the draft will upload any iterative draft updates directly within the Slack thread as adjustments are made.

### Stage 5: Final Review and Approval
Finally, the writer of the draft will review and analyze all the feedback received by editing and updating the draft. Once the 48-hour period is complete, the draft must be formally approved by **Bree or approved faculty** before the draft is posted live on LinkedIn.

## Outputs or Success Criteria
*   **Approved Posts:** A polished and  approved "Before" or "After" external event post ready for LinkedIn publication.
*   **Contained Feedback:** Edits and discussions are contained within the dedicated Slack draft thread.
